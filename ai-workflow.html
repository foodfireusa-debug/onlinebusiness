How AI Can Improve a Workflow

A practical, beginner-friendly guide with clear steps, useful considerations, and responsible best practices.

Find Repetitive Work

Look for tasks such as summarizing notes, drafting routine messages, organizing information, or creating first drafts. These are often good candidates for assistance.

Keep the Human Decision

Automation should support judgment, not remove it from important decisions. Define which steps can be automated and which require review.

Measure the Benefit

Compare time saved, error rates, cost, and output quality before and after the change. An automated workflow is useful only if it improves the overall process.

Protect Data

Review what information is being sent to an AI service and whether it is appropriate to share. Keep confidential information out of tools unless their terms and your permissions support the use.
