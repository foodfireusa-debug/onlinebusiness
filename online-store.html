Starting an Online Store

An online store sells physical or digital products through a website or marketplace. The first step is deciding what customer problem the product solves and who is most likely to buy it.

Research Before Spending

Compare competitors, pricing, product quality, shipping expectations, and customer reviews. Testing demand with a small product range can reduce unnecessary upfront costs.

Create a Trustworthy Store

Use accurate product descriptions, clear pricing, understandable shipping information, contact details, and a straightforward return or refund policy where applicable.

Improve Over Time

Track which products attract attention and which actually produce sales. Use customer questions and feedback to improve descriptions, photos, product selection, and support.
