What Are Digital Products?

Digital products are files or resources that can be delivered online, such as checklists, templates, worksheets, guides, planners, and educational resources.

Choose a Useful Problem

Start with a specific problem. For example, a small business owner may need a social-media content calendar, while a job seeker may need a simple application checklist. The clearer the problem, the easier it is to design a useful resource.

Create Quality Resources

Make the instructions easy to follow, organize the file logically, proofread it, and include examples where they improve understanding. A small, genuinely useful resource can be more valuable than a large collection of generic pages.

Trust and Updates

Describe exactly what the product contains and avoid exaggerated claims. If information can become outdated, create a process for reviewing and updating the resource.
