AI for Research

AI research tools can help organize questions, summarize documents, compare ideas, and identify topics that need further investigation.

Ask Focused Questions

Break a broad research task into smaller questions. Ask for evidence, definitions, assumptions, and areas of uncertainty instead of accepting a confident answer automatically.

Verify Sources

Treat AI output as a starting point. Check original sources for important facts, numbers, quotations, and current information.

Create a Research Record

Keep notes about which sources you checked and what conclusions they support. This makes later review easier and reduces accidental repetition of errors.
