AI in Customer Support

AI support tools can help answer common questions, classify requests, draft responses, and route conversations to the right team.

Start With Common Questions

Create a reliable knowledge base for frequent questions and define which requests require a human. Good source information is essential.

Keep Humans in the Loop

Escalate billing disputes, sensitive issues, unusual requests, and situations where the automated system is uncertain.

Measure Quality

Track resolution time, escalation rate, customer satisfaction, and recurring mistakes. Improve the underlying documentation when the same problem appears repeatedly.
