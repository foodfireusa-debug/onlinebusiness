Validate Before You Invest

A practical, beginner-friendly guide with clear steps, useful considerations, and responsible best practices.

Why Validation Matters

Validation reduces the risk of building something nobody wants. It does not require perfect certainty; it means collecting evidence before committing significant time or money.

Talk to Potential Users

Ask people about their current problem, how they solve it now, and what they find difficult. Listen for repeated needs rather than trying to persuade them to like your idea.

Build a Small Test

A simple landing page, sample service, prototype, or small paid pilot can reveal whether people take meaningful action.

Learn From Results

If the test performs poorly, change the offer, audience, or approach instead of automatically spending more. Evidence should guide the next step.
