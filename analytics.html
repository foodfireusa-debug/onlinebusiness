Analytics

A practical, beginner-friendly guide with clear steps, useful considerations, and responsible best practices.

Why Analytics Matters

Analytics helps you understand how people interact with a website or campaign. It can show which pages attract visitors and where users stop progressing.

Choose Useful Metrics

Start with metrics connected to your goal. For a content site, engaged visits and returning readers may matter; for a lead page, completed inquiries may be more important.

Use Data as Evidence

Look for patterns rather than reacting to a single day's numbers. Compare similar periods and investigate possible causes before making major changes.

Protect User Privacy

Use analytics responsibly, disclose relevant data practices, and configure tracking according to applicable laws and platform requirements.
