Workflow Automation

A practical, beginner-friendly guide with clear steps, useful considerations, and responsible best practices.

What AI Automation Means

AI automation connects tools and processes so repetitive work can happen with less manual effort. Examples include summarizing forms, routing messages, or creating draft reports.

Map the Process First

Write down the current workflow before automating it. Identify inputs, decisions, outputs, and exceptions.

Add Review Points

Automate low-risk repetitive steps first. Keep human approval for decisions that could cause financial, legal, privacy, or customer harm.

Monitor the System

Check logs, error rates, outputs, and costs. Automation should be reviewed regularly rather than assumed to work perfectly forever.
