How to Build a Simple Portfolio

A practical, beginner-friendly guide with clear steps, useful considerations, and responsible best practices.

Show Relevant Work

A portfolio should make it easy to understand what you can do. Choose a small set of strong examples that match the type of work you want.

Explain Each Example

For each project, describe the goal, your role, the process, and the result where you can do so honestly. Context is often more useful than a screenshot alone.

Keep It Simple

Use clear navigation and readable pages. Visitors should quickly find your services, examples, contact method, and basic background.

Update Regularly

Replace weaker examples with stronger work as your skills improve. Keep claims accurate and avoid presenting someone else's work as your own.
