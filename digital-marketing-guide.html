Digital Marketing Guide

A beginner-friendly overview of the main digital marketing channels.

SEO

Search engine optimization helps useful pages become easier for search engines and users to discover.

Content

Good content answers a real question or solves a real problem. Original examples and clear explanations add value.

Social

Choose a platform that matches your audience and create a consistent content system instead of posting randomly.

Email

Use permission-based lists and provide useful information. Respect unsubscribe requests and applicable laws.

Analytics

Measure traffic sources, engagement and conversions. Use the data to identify what deserves more attention.
