AI Video Workflows

AI can assist with scripts, storyboards, captions, editing ideas, voice work, and visual generation. The strongest workflow usually combines automation with human review.

Start With the Story

Define the audience, purpose, hook, main points, and call to action before generating visuals. A clear story matters more than using many effects.

Edit for Clarity

Remove unnecessary sections, check captions, balance audio, and make sure the final video works on the target platform and screen size.

Check Rights and Accuracy

Review generated assets and voices for permitted use and avoid presenting synthetic material as authentic evidence.
