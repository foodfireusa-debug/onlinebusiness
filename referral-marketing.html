What Referral Marketing Is

Referral marketing encourages existing customers or partners to introduce suitable new customers. It can work through recommendations, referral programs, partnerships, or affiliate relationships.

Make the Offer Clear

Explain who qualifies, what action is required, and how any reward works. Terms should be easy to understand before someone participates.

Prioritize Fit

A referral is valuable when the new customer is genuinely suitable. Incentives should not encourage misleading claims or spam.

Track Results

Record referral sources, completed actions, and costs. Use the information to identify which partnerships produce valuable customers.
